01 Professional Summary

I build AI tools that real teams use every day, not demos. At GEMMACON North America, an automotive software consultancy, I find the slow, manual parts of how we run the business and replace them with software I write and ship myself, including agentic research pipelines, document automation, and containerized Python services. I start by sitting with the people doing the work to pin down what they actually need, then I build it so they can't easily get it wrong.
